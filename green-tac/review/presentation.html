Problem and objectives

Extra compilation work can pay off when a program runs often.

  1. Generate TAC from a small C-like language.
  2. Compare three optimization strategies.
  3. Choose a strategy using compilation and runtime carbon costs.
  4. Apply a predicted runtime-overhead limit.
